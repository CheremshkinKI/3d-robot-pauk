Model & interpretation

Coordinates use Z up, in meters. Complex ray superposition includes a direct path, six image-source reflections and four voltage-controlled scatterers. Voltage-to-phase is linear and idealized. Sensor packets are sampled at 200 Hz in simulation time.

The 3 dB peak-to-peak gate is intentionally strict: ordinary 1.5 dB Gaussian noise can also fail it. The final result is the best stable observed point, not a proof of a global maximum. A 1 m cube at 0.121 m wavelength has many fine interference ripples; 25 cm exploration can miss peaks. Scatterer gain is calculated, never forced to +3–6 dB.

Mechanical motion is a kinematic illustration with damped oscillation, not a tension or collision solver. Cable robots require a feasible tension region. All RSSI values are synthetic; no hardware connection or calibration.

Measurement protocol

Move → settle → 20 packets → median-assisted Kalman → stability gate. Unstable samples never update the stable optimum. Changing sensor, scenario, strategy or mode restarts the experiment.
